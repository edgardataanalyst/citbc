Charity and Community Service

Love • Care • Serve

Serving Others with the Love of Christ

The Charity and Community Service Ministry puts Christian compassion into action by caring for people and responding to needs in the community. Through acts of kindness, giving, and practical service, the church seeks to reflect the love and grace of Christ.

We believe that serving others is an opportunity to demonstrate God's love in practical ways. Whether through helping those in need, offering encouragement, or supporting community efforts, every act of kindness can make a meaningful difference.
