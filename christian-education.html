Our Ministry

Christian Education

Growing in the knowledge of God's Word and learning to live out His truth every day.

Learn • Grow • Live the Word

Grounded in God's Word

The Christian Education Ministry focuses on teaching and learning the Word of God. Through Bible study, discipleship, and biblical instruction, believers are encouraged to grow in their knowledge of Scripture and apply God's truth in everyday life.

Our desire is to help individuals develop a stronger understanding of the Bible, deepen their faith, and continue growing in their walk with the Lord.
