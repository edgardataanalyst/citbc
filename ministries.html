SERVING GOD, SERVING OTHERS

Ministries of Christ Is Truth Baptist Church International.

The ministries of Christ Is Truth Baptist Church International are established to fulfill the Church's biblical mandate to win lost souls, strengthen lives, and faithfully serve the Lord until Christ's Second Coming. Recognizing the diverse cultural backgrounds, economic conditions, and social realities of Filipino communities in urban settings, the Church seeks to provide a Christ-centered ministry that is spiritually enriching, socially responsive, and accessible to people from different walks of life.

With particular concern for the neighboring individuals, families, children, young people, and indigent households, each ministry proclaims the Gospel, nurtures Christian character, strengthens relationships, and encourages humble service to God and others.
