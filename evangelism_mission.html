Making Christ Known

The Evangelism and Mission Ministry is committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and reaching people with the message of God's saving grace. It encourages believers to take part in outreach, witness to others, and support the mission of making Christ known.

Through personal testimony, gospel sharing, and opportunities to serve beyond the church, we seek to point people to the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

Ways We Share the Gospel

Every believer can take part in sharing the good news and showing the love of Christ through words and actions.

✝

Gospel Sharing

Sharing the message of salvation through Jesus Christ with people in our families, communities, and beyond.

◎

Community Outreach

Connecting with people in the community and creating opportunities to share God's Word and love.

♡

Compassion in Action

Showing Christ's love through kindness, encouragement, practical help, and genuine care for others.

⌖

Personal Witness

Encouraging believers to share their faith and personal testimony with gentleness and respect.

Making Disciples
