Worship Ministry

Gathering together in praise and worship, lifting our voices and hearts to glorify God.

Worship Together. Glorify God.

The Worship Ministry is dedicated to encouraging the church to worship God with sincere hearts. Through congregational singing, music, and special worship gatherings, we come together to honor the Lord and grow in our relationship with Him.

Whether through our regular Sunday service or special celebrations throughout the year, everyone is welcome to join us as we worship and give glory to God.
