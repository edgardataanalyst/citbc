Our Ministry

Family, Children and Youth

Nurturing faith, building strong families, and guiding the next generation to know and follow Christ.

Nurture • Guide • Grow Together

Growing in Faith at Every Stage

The Family, Children and Youth Ministry seeks to nurture faith and strengthen relationships among families, children, and young people. Through age-appropriate biblical learning, guidance, and church activities, each generation is encouraged to know Christ and grow in faith.

We believe that every generation has a place in the life of the church. Our desire is to encourage families, help children learn God's Word, and guide young people as they grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ.
