GET TO KNOW US

About Identity

We are the called-out ones, people whom God has called out of darkness into His marvelous light. We are redeemed sinners, saved by God’s grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, and brought into the body of Christ as His people. Through a personal encounter with the Gospel, we have repented of our sins and placed our faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior. Made new in Christ, we no longer live according to our former sinful nature but are called to live a transformed life that reflects Christ in our character, conduct, relationships, and purpose. Our identity is no longer defined by who we were, but by whom we have become in Christ: forgiven, redeemed, transformed, and called to belong to Him and live for His glory.

OUR BEGINNINGS

Our Seed Project 2010

During this time, Pastor Paul Tanchi, son of Pastor Peter Tanchi of Christ’s Commission Fellowship (CCF), envisioned expanding God’s Kingdom by initiating Bible studies in various AXEIA project sites—an outreach ministry known as the “Seed Project.” Seeking to nurture spiritual growth among construction workers stationed in their subdivision developments, he looked for pastors who could lead these Bible study gatherings. One of these project sites was Alta Tierra Homes in General Mariano Alvarez, Cavite. During the pioneering stage of the ministry, Engineer Pedro Mones worked alongside and assisted Pastor Domingo Ranay, helping lay the groundwork for what would eventually become a growing church.

To support this mission, AXEIA coordinated with a nearby local church, Upon This Rock Fundamental Baptist Church in Monterverde Homes, led by the late Reverend David Sariga. Responding to the call, Pastor Sariga appointed Pastor Domingo Ranay to conduct regular Bible studies for the construction workers at the Alta Tierra project site. When the construction phase concluded and the workers were reassigned to other project locations, the Bible study did not end. Instead, it continued for the residents who had begun attending and expressed a desire to learn God’s Word. What started as a simple weekly Bible instruction gradually developed into a consistent Sunday morning worship gathering. Through faithful Gospel preaching, discipleship, and outreach, several residents came to know Christ, families joined the fellowship, and the ministry steadily grew. ​ In 2013, the congregation celebrated its first anniversary, marking its emergence as a thriving local church. Recognizing the need for organizational strength and legal identity, the church was formally registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in 2019, solidifying its status as an independent religious organization.
